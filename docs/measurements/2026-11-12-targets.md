# The engine against RocksDB and SplinterDB on each supported target, with the cache-loan fix

2026-11-12, mantle `6398ad28` (branch validate-targets-2: main `87ab09e1`, rustix 1.1.6, and the
cache-loan fix of [2026-11-11-wrong-reads.md](2026-11-11-wrong-reads.md)). Carries
[2026-11-06-targets.md](2026-11-06-targets.md) forward. Supported targets: Linux and Windows on
x86_64 and aarch64, macOS on aarch64 (the owner: macOS x86_64 is not supported).

**Question.** On each supported target, does the engine hold 4-5x over RocksDB and SplinterDB on
fills, gets and seeks at 10M and 100M keys, and do metadata replication and transfer work?

**Answer, like for like.** Every arm under one memory cap, the comparators' 16 GiB cache and their
larger measured overhead (17,117 MiB; § Equal memory), Linux aarch64, ten rounds a size:

| under 17,117 MiB, n=10 | 10M fills | 10M gets | 10M seeks | 100M fills | 100M gets | 100M seeks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| main (`6398ad28`) over RocksDB | 5.23x | 9.95x | 7.89x | 3.69x | **0.31x** | 0.85x |
| the kept port (`31123e68`) over RocksDB | 6.07x | 13.12x | 7.62x | 4.90x | **0.38x** | 0.96x |
| main over SplinterDB | 3.90x | 3.18x | 4.64x | 2.68x | 1.00x | 3.67x |
| the kept port over SplinterDB | 4.43x | 4.06x | 4.51x | 3.49x | 1.16x | 4.50x |

At 100M mantle answers about a third of RocksDB's gets. **The 100M gets ratios in the tables after
this one were not like for like**: they gave mantle the machine's memory (`--machine-memory`, its
kept memtables about 20 GB at 100M) and the comparators 16 GiB caches. The fills and the 10M ratios
hold either way. What separates a 100M get, and the work on it, is in § Equal memory.

## How it ran

The owner's harness (`benchmark-results/mantle-splinterdb-20261013`: `inside7.sh`,
`build-inner.sh`, `splinter_bench.c`, `bench-image.Dockerfile`), as 2026-11-06 ran it: on Linux in
its container, on macOS by `mac_williams.py` (the same design and flags; SplinterDB's I/O is
Linux-only, so macOS has no SplinterDB arm). Arms interleaved in a Williams order, each case's
rusage kept beside its output, its store removed after. Every arm: 16-byte keys, 100-byte values,
seed 301, a fill of NUM puts, 1M gets, 1M seeks of 10 rows, one client, no WAL, no compression,
64 MiB memtables.

- mantle: `benches/ranges.rs DB NUM 1 1000000 256 16 1000000 10 --rocks-seed=301 --shard-client
  --segments --machine-memory`, a release build with no diagnostic code; each case's values checked
  against the fill's oracle and its reopened store verified key by key.
- `rocks`, `rocks16`, `rocks32`: `db_bench` 11.8.1 (`abeebd963`), 256 MiB, 16 GiB and 32 GiB block
  cache, `--max_background_jobs` at the cores.
- `sdb16all`: `splinter_bench` over SplinterDB `7d607b11`, 16 GiB cache, every other core in the
  background.

shared among a handle's duplicates, `2026-11-06-targets-hyper-block-fd.patch`) waits on Ada's word.

is the next run's question (THP counters sampled beside the same two cases). This run's held
degradation was smaller than 19:50Z's (gets 0.72-0.83M then), which the Mac's other work moves.

and `/proc/vmstat`'s THP counters sampled every 2 s: quiet, 23.3-24.5 GB of mantle's 25.6-26.9 GB of

mantle degrades where RocksDB and SplinterDB, whose costs a get are dominated elsewhere, barely move.
